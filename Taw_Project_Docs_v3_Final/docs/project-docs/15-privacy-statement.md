# Taw — Privacy Statement Draft

> **Status:** working publication draft. Update it to match the production implementation, hosting region, vendors and contact details before public release. This is a product draft, not legal advice.

## 1. Scope

Taw ("توّ") provides software that allows water stations and distributors to operate branded online ordering and delivery services. This privacy statement describes the categories of information the Taw platform may process when customers, distributors, employees and drivers use the service.

## 2. Information that may be collected

Depending on the feature being used, Taw may process:

- Name.
- Phone number.
- Email address when provided.
- Delivery address and address notes.
- Geographic coordinates when a user chooses to share a location for delivery.
- Order details and order history.
- Water subscription details.
- Reusable-bottle delivery/return balance and transaction history.
- Support requests and customer notes.
- Driver/employee account and operational information.
- Device/browser, push-subscription and technical diagnostic information.
- Authentication/verification metadata when OTP or connected identity is used.

Taw should not require unnecessary personal data for a guest water order.

## 3. Why information is processed

Information may be used to:

- Create and fulfill water orders.
- Deliver orders to the requested location.
- Manage recurring water subscriptions.
- Allow a distributor to manage customers, drivers and delivery schedules.
- Track reusable water bottles.
- Send transactional notifications about orders and deliveries.
- Send optional marketing/offers only where the relevant settings/consent allow it.
- Provide customer support.
- Protect accounts and prevent abuse.
- Diagnose failures, monitor performance and improve the product.

## 4. Guest ordering

Taw is designed to allow customer ordering without requiring a full account where the connected water distributor enables this feature. A phone number and delivery information may still be required to fulfill the order.

Protected account information, subscription management and other sensitive customer views may require phone verification or another approved authentication method.

## 5. Location information

Location is used only where needed for delivery-related functionality. The customer may be offered an option to share the current/map location or enter an address manually, depending on distributor configuration.

Precise location must not be collected continuously for ordinary customers.

Driver location tracking, if introduced later, must be documented separately and reflected in the production privacy statement and consent/permission flow.

## 6. Notifications and communications

Taw may support:

- Push notifications.
- WhatsApp links or, later, approved WhatsApp Business messaging.
- Email/SMS if enabled in future.

Transactional messages and marketing messages should be treated separately where the channel/platform requires distinct consent or settings.

## 7. Service providers

Taw may rely on third-party providers for hosting, maps/navigation, telemetry, push notifications, OTP/SMS, email, WhatsApp Business or other infrastructure. The production version of this statement should list or describe the relevant categories/providers when selected.

## 8. Data access

A water distributor using Taw can access the customer and operational data associated with its own tenant/store. Taw's multi-tenant design must prevent one distributor from accessing another distributor's data.

Authorized Taw platform administrators may access limited information when necessary for support, security or platform operation.

## 9. Retention

Retention periods should be finalized before production based on actual operational/legal needs. Orders and transactional records may need to be retained longer than temporary technical logs.

Production configuration should define retention for:

- Orders and delivery history.
- Customer records.
- Bottle ledger records.
- Support requests.
- Audit logs.
- Application/telemetry logs.

## 10. Security

Taw should use appropriate technical and organizational safeguards, including tenant isolation, backend authorization, secure secret management, encrypted transport and restricted production access.

Passwords, tokens, OTP values and private credentials must never be intentionally written to application logs.

## 11. User choices

Where applicable, users may be able to:

- Update account/profile information.
- Manage saved addresses.
- Control optional marketing notifications.
- Pause/modify supported subscriptions.
- Contact the distributor or Taw support regarding personal information.

## 12. Children

Taw is not designed as a service specifically directed at children. Production legal requirements should be reviewed for the countries where the platform is launched.

## 13. Changes

This statement may be updated as features, vendors, legal requirements or data practices change. The current publication date/version should be shown on the public privacy page.
