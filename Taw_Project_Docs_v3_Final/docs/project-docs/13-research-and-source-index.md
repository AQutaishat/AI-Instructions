# Taw — Research and Source Index

This file lists important external/public sources used during product discovery. It is not a substitute for refreshing time-sensitive numbers before public claims.

## Jordan consumer water / delivery competitors

### Qatra

Google Play:

https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.qatra.waterdelivery

Public description includes water ordering and flexible recurring subscriptions.

### Zad — Water & Gas Delivery

App Store Jordan:

https://apps.apple.com/jo/app/zad-water-gas-delivery/id6799771203

Public description includes water/gas delivery, map-based address, tracking and payment capabilities.

## Jordan water distributor SaaS

### WaterCopon

Main:

https://watercopon.com/

Features:

https://www.watercopon.com/features

Pricing:

https://watercopon.com/pricing

Terms:

https://watercopon.com/terms

Public research in September 2026 showed WaterCopon positioning itself as SaaS for Jordanian/regional water distributors with digital water coupon/customer-management features, PWA use, WhatsApp notifications, reports and progressively broader operational/marketing features.

Pricing, plan limits, download counts, review counts and other fast-changing market data should always be rechecked before external use.
