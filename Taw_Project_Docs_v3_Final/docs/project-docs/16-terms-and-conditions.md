# Taw — Terms and Conditions Draft

> **Status:** working publication draft. Review and adapt before public release. This is a product draft, not legal advice.

## 1. About Taw

Taw ("توّ") is software that allows water stations and distributors to publish a branded ordering storefront and manage water products, customer orders, subscriptions, delivery operations, drivers and reusable-bottle records.

Unless explicitly stated otherwise, Taw is the software platform and is not itself the seller, producer or physical delivery provider for water sold by an independent distributor using the platform.

## 2. Distributor responsibility

Each water station/distributor is responsible for its own:

- Products and product descriptions.
- Prices and delivery fees.
- Water quality and regulatory compliance.
- Delivery areas and promised schedules.
- Drivers/employees.
- Customer service.
- Refund/cancellation policies where applicable.
- Tax/invoice/legal obligations.

## 3. Customer orders

Customers may be able to place orders without creating a full account. Required details may include name, phone number, requested products and delivery address/location.

An order may be subject to confirmation, delivery availability, minimum-order rules and distributor-specific settings.

## 4. Water subscriptions

Where enabled, recurring delivery subscriptions may allow customers to select quantity, frequency, day and preferred delivery time.

Depending on distributor settings, a customer may be allowed to skip, pause, resume or modify future deliveries. Generated delivery orders remain subject to operational availability and the distributor's current rules.

## 5. Reusable bottles

Where reusable bottles are tracked, the platform may record bottles delivered, empties returned and resulting balances. The distributor is responsible for the commercial rules associated with deposits, missing bottles or adjustments.

## 6. Accounts and verification

Some functionality may require authentication or phone verification. Users are responsible for protecting their accounts and verification channels.

Taw may restrict or suspend access when necessary for security, abuse prevention or platform integrity.

## 7. Availability

Taw aims to provide reliable service but does not guarantee uninterrupted availability. Maintenance, third-party failures, connectivity issues or other events may temporarily affect the service.

## 8. Maps, communications and third parties

Features may use third-party maps/navigation, push services, WhatsApp, email/SMS providers, identity providers, hosting or AI/MCP clients. Third-party services may have their own terms and privacy rules.

## 9. Free service and future plans

The initial Taw product is intended to remain free for an extended adoption period. Taw may later introduce paid plans or optional paid capabilities. Any such change should be communicated before charges apply to an existing user.
